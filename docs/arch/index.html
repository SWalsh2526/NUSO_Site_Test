About NUSO.

Northwestern University Science Olympiad was founded in 2021 with a mission to better our surrounding Science Olympiad community.

Composed of Science Olympiad alums and STEM-loving folks, we work year-round to bring quality competition to our Evanston campus and create memorable experiences for students.
